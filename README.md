# Lumina Forge – The Artisan’s Toolkit for City-Building Sandboxes

Welcome to **Lumina Forge**, a community-driven enhancement suite designed for immersive city-building and life-simulation experiences. This project is not a simple utility; it is a philosophical approach to reshaping how players interact with their digital worlds. Instead of short-term exploits, Lumina Forge offers a curated set of **creative conveniences** that expand the boundaries of your virtual canvas, allowing you to sculpt stories and environments with unprecedented fluidity.

Built for enthusiasts who view the game engine as a form of digital pottery, Lumina Forge provides a suite of tools that feel less like a command center and more like a master artisan’s workshop. Whether you are a meticulous architect, a social storyteller, or a chaotic deity testing the limits of physics, this toolkit is crafted to feel intuitive, responsive, and unobtrusive. We believe in empowering the player, not overriding the game. This repository houses the complete source code, design documentation, and localization files for the project, ensuring complete transparency and collaborative growth.

## 🎨 Overview: Crafting Beyond the Grid

The core philosophy behind Lumina Forge is **"Responsive Opulence"** – the idea that a rich experience should not require tedious resource grinding. We provide a series of **quality-of-life amplifiers** that let you bypass the mundane aspects of resource management, not to diminish the challenge, but to amplify your creative output. Think of it as a "Renaissance Patronage" system; you provide the vision, and Lumina Forge provides the funding and the tools to manifest it instantly.

Our interface is designed as a **Spatial Command Deck**, a non-intrusive overlay that feels like a natural extension of the game’s UI. It is not a clunky external program; it is a sleek, holographic panel that slides into view with a keypress, offering context-sensitive options based on your current activity. This ensures that the flow of creation is never broken by alt-tabbing or navigating complex menus.

![Interface Concept](https://img.shields.io/badge/UI-Concept-00A98F)

### Why "Forge"?
Because we don't "hack" or "break" the game; we *forge* new pathways through it. We kneel at the anvil of the engine’s rules and hammer out new possibilities. The result is a suite of **legitimate in-game enhancements** that feel organic to the world, avoiding the rigidity of traditional mods by focusing on dynamic, real-time adjustments.

## 🚀 Getting Started: Igniting the Forge

Before you can paint with light, you need to prime the canvas. Setting up Lumina Forge is designed to be as seamless as the tool itself. We prioritize **Plug-and-Play Integration**; there are no complex scripts to run or environments to configure manually. You simply place the module into your designated game directory and launch the game.

### Prerequisites
- A modern operating system (Windows 10/11, macOS 14+, or a mainstream Linux distribution).
- The base game installed with the latest Early Access patch.
- A desire to explore the untapped potential of your city.

### Installation Ritual
1.  **Acquire the Module:** Download the latest release archive from the section below.
2.  **Place the Keystone:** Extract the contents into your main game directory, ensuring the `.forge` folder sits alongside the main executable.
3.  **First Ignition:** Launch the game. The Forge will automatically detect the environment and initialize itself. You will see a subtle shimmer in the corner of your screen indicating readiness.
4.  **Binding the Controls:** Access the Configuration Panel (default key: `F8`) to customize your command bindings and interface theme.

## ✨ Feature Compendium: The Artisan’s Arsenal

This section details the granular tools available within the Forge. Each feature is developed with a focus on **stability** and **immersive integration**.

### 💰 The Golden Ledger (Resource Management)
- **Infinite Patronage:** Toggle a continuous, high-yield trickle of in-game currency. This is designed to feel like a steady stream of royal support rather than a sudden explosion of wealth.
- **Material Phasing:** Instantly phase-alter building materials to their highest quality state, bypassing upgrade timers.
- **Market Manipulation:** Adjust the baseline cost of goods to zero or negative, effectively creating a seller's market that benefits your treasury.

### 🏗️ Architectural Geometry (Build Mode Enhancements)
- **Gravity Agnosticism:** Toggle gravity off for selected objects, allowing for floating structures and impossible geometries. Perfect for creating ethereal, sky-bound districts.
- **Temporal Fast-Forward:** Accelerate construction timers to instant completion, watching your skyline rise in a matter of seconds.
- **Placement Override:** Rotate objects on all three axes with precision controls, unlocking 360-degree placement freedom.

### 🧬 Social Alchemy (Character & Life Management)
- **Mood Lattice:** Adjust the emotional state of any Parafolk to a "Sublime" condition, ensuring they always perform at their peak.
- **Vitality Wellspring:** Reinforce a character's biological resilience, reducing the impact of fatigue, hunger, and social strain. This is a state of *optimal equilibrium*.
- **Relationship Weaving:** Instantly reweave the social fabric between characters, setting relationship levels to "Cherished" or "Friendly."

### 🧠 Utility Nexus (System Enhancements)
- **Stealth Utils:** Hide the interface overlay entirely for screenshots and recording, enabling a pristine view of your creation.
- **Save State Branching:** Create "Fork Points" in your save game, allowing you to experiment with divergent timelines without permanently altering your main world.
- **Telemetry Console:** A full diagnostic list of active modules and performance metrics to ensure the Forge runs efficiently.
